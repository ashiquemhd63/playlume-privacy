Playlume – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Playlume (“the app”) is a piano learning app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Playlume does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps these things in its own private storage on your phone, so it can work:

  • MIDI files you choose to import, and the songs made from them
  • your practice history (practice time, notes played, accuracy, finished songs)
  • your settings (for example your daily goal, theme, and the optional name you type for the greeting)

This information never leaves your phone through Playlume. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

What the app does not do

  • no account or sign-in
  • no advertising and no advertising ID
  • no analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools
  • no access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone
  • no sale or sharing of data with anyone

Files you import

Playlume only opens a file when you pick it in Android’s file picker or share it to the app. It keeps a copy of that file in its own storage so the song can be changed later. Please import only music you have the right to use.

Backups and moving to a new phone

The app’s data is not uploaded to cloud backups. When you move to a new phone using a cable or a direct phone-to-phone transfer (Android 9 or newer), Android may copy the app’s data to the new phone. That transfer is done by Android, not by Playlume, and does not go through any server of ours.

Deleting your data

You can delete a song in the app (Library → ⋯ → Delete from this phone); its practice history stays. To delete everything, including your practice history and settings, uninstall Playlume or use Android Settings → Apps → Playlume → Storage → Clear storage.

Children

Playlume does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
